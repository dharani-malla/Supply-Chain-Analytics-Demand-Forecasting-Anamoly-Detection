## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze historical supply chain data using time-series analytics to identify demand patterns, detect anomalies, forecast future product demand, and optimize inventory management. The project aims to help businesses make data-driven decisions, reduce stockouts, minimize excess inventory, and improve overall supply chain efficiency.

## Project Description
Efficient supply chain management relies on understanding demand patterns and maintaining optimal inventory levels. This project focuses on analyzing historical supply chain data through time-series analysis, anomaly detection, and demand forecasting. Time-series decomposition is used to identify trend, seasonality, and residual components, while statistical techniques detect unusual demand fluctuations. Forecasting models are then applied to predict future demand, enabling businesses to optimize inventory, reduce operational costs, and improve customer satisfaction. The project concludes with an interactive Streamlit dashboard that visualizes demand trends, anomalies, forecasts, and key business insights.

## Key Concepts

### 1.Time-Series Resampling
Time-series resampling changes the frequency of time-based data, such as converting daily observations into weekly or monthly aggregates. It helps analyze demand trends at different time intervals.

### 2.Time-Series Decomposition
Time-series decomposition separates historical demand data into Trend, Seasonality, and Residual components to better understand underlying demand patterns.

### 3.Trend Analysis
Trend analysis identifies the long-term movement in demand over time, helping businesses understand whether demand is increasing, decreasing, or remaining stable.

### 4.Seasonality Analysis
Seasonality analysis identifies recurring patterns in demand that occur at regular intervals, such as weekly, monthly, or yearly cycles.

### 5.Anomaly Detection
Anomaly detection identifies unusual demand or inventory patterns, such as sudden spikes or unexpected drops, allowing businesses to detect operational issues quickly.

### 6.Demand Forecasting
Demand forecasting predicts future product demand using historical data, enabling organizations to make informed inventory and production decisions.

### 7.Inventory Optimization
Inventory optimization ensures that the right quantity of products is available at the right time while minimizing storage costs and reducing stock shortages.

### 8.Time-Series Visualization
Time-series visualization uses charts and graphs to display historical demand, trends, seasonal patterns, anomalies, and forecasts, making business insights easier to interpret.

### 9.Forecast Evaluation
Forecast evaluation measures the accuracy of forecasting models using metrics such as Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE).

### 10.Business Decision Support
The analytical insights generated from forecasting and anomaly detection help organizations optimize inventory planning, improve operational efficiency, reduce costs, and enhance customer satisfaction.
