# Week 1 Progress Report

## Project Title
Supply Chain Analytics: Time-Series Analysis, Demand Forecasting, and Inventory Optimization

## Introduction
The objective of this project is to analyze retail inventory and sales data using time-series analytics to identify demand patterns, perform trend and seasonal analysis, and 
generate insights for demand forecasting and inventory optimization. The project aims to support data-driven inventory management and improve supply chain decision-making.

## Week 1 Progress Summary
During Week 1, the team focused on project planning, repository setup, documentation, dataset understanding, and exploratory time-series analysis. The primary goal was to establish a strong analytical foundation by preparing the dataset, visualizing sales trends, and understanding demand behavior through various time-series techniques.

## Work Completed

### 1. Project Setup and Documentation
- Created and organized the GitHub repository for collaborative development.
- Documented project objectives, scope, and team details.
- Prepared documentation on Time-Series Analysis, Time-Series Resampling, Time-Series Decomposition, Demand Forecasting, Inventory Optimization, and Anomaly Detection.
- Documented dataset information, project methodology, and feature descriptions.

### 2. Dataset Selection and Understanding
- Selected the Retail Store Inventory Dataset.
- Studied the dataset structure, attributes, and business relevance.
- Identified key variables required for time-series analysis, including Date, Units Sold, Inventory Level, and Demand Forecast.
- Documented the dataset schema and business importance of each feature.

### 3. Data Preparation
- Uploaded the cleaned dataset to the project repository.
- Converted the Date column to datetime format.
- Sorted records chronologically.
- Aggregated daily sales data for time-series analysis.
- Prepared the dataset for visualization and further analysis.

### 4. Time-Series Visualization
- Generated the original daily units sold time-series visualization.
- Performed weekly sales trend analysis.
- Performed monthly sales trend analysis.
- Compared weekly and monthly demand patterns using visualizations.
- Documented observations and business interpretations.

### 5. Time-Series Decomposition
- Performed additive time-series decomposition using the Statsmodels library.
- Visualized Trend, Seasonal, and Residual components.
- Interpreted each component to understand long-term trends, recurring seasonal patterns, and random fluctuations.

### 6. Rolling Statistics Analysis
- Calculated the 30-day rolling mean.
- Calculated the 30-day rolling standard deviation.
- Visualized rolling statistics to analyze demand stability and variability.
- Documented key observations and business insights.

## Key Insights and Learnings
- Time-series visualization provides a clear understanding of historical demand behavior.
- Weekly and monthly aggregation reveal short-term and long-term sales patterns.
- Time-series decomposition separates trend, seasonal, and residual components for better analysis.
- Rolling statistics help evaluate demand stability and fluctuations over time.
- Proper data preparation is essential for accurate forecasting and anomaly detection.
